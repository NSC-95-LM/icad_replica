Frequently Asked Questions
and Answers

  • What are the courses offered by ICAD?

    ICAD offers specialized courses for JEE (Main & Advanced) Exclusive JEE Main and MHT-CET, NEET, and Foundation programs to build a strong academic base.

  • What is the success rate of ICAD in JEE and NEET exams?

    ICAD has consistently produced top rankers in JEE and NEET with a selection ratio of over 50%, hence placing thousands of students in IITs / NITs / AIIMS and Government Medical Colleges.

  • What is the fee structure for ICAD courses?

    ICAD provides detailed fee structures for its courses, which vary depending on the program and duration. Scholarships are also available. These are required to be paid in 2 to 4 installments again depending on the course.

  • How can I apply for ICAD's scholarship programs?

    Scholarships can be availed through the LEAP Exam (National Level Exam) or based on academic performance of previous classes and entrance test CPA conducted by ICAD.

  • Does ICAD provide online coaching for JEE and NEET?

    No, ICAD strongly believes in the need of physical classroom training as these exams require a focused approach throughout

  • Where are ICAD's coaching centers located?

    ICAD centers are located across Maharashtra primarily in Nagpur, Amravati, Chandrapur, Yavatmal, Nashik, Nanded, and Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar. You can find the nearest center on the official website www.icad.org.in

  • What is the CPA Exam by ICAD, and how can I participate?

    The CPA Exam by ICAD is a scholarship test where students can earn up to 90% fee waivers and other benefits. Visit the ICAD website for registration details and start your journey to success!

  • What is the difference between ICAD's Rank Booster Program and regular courses?

    The Rank Booster Program focuses on intensive preparation and revision, aimed at improving scores in JEE and NEET.

  • What is the eligibility criteria to join ICAD?

    Eligibility varies by course; students from Class 8 to 12 or those preparing for competitive exams can enroll.

  • How do I get detailed information about ICAD's batches and schedules?

    Information about batches and schedules is available on the ICAD website ( www.icad.org.in) or through ICAD admissions office.
